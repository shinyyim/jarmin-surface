Structure · how the surface is shaped

Inbox
A list of items, triage first. Open one to see it.
Why not: scales to forty, but the conversation has nowhere to live and the detail pane becomes a second app. It reads as homework.
Chat-first
One thread with the assistant, work rendered inline.
Why not: talking is natural, but thirty-one items in a scrollback is unmanageable and every reply needs an antecedent, "which email?"
Split
Queue on the left, thread on the right.
Why not: both jobs visible, but two places to look and every reply means choosing a side. Twice the surface for someone with coffee.
Adaptive surface· kept
One column of cards. The conversation lives inside the card it is about, and a standing composer takes everything else.
Why: approvals and questions are different jobs but the same shape of interruption, and "make this warmer" needs no antecedent when typed under the draft. The assistant groups the heavy day before you arrive. See DECISIONS.md D-004, D-006, D-007.
